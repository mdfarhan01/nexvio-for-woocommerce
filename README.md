## ✨ Overview

**Nexvio for WooCommerce** is a WordPress plugin that extends Elementor with a collection of customizable WooCommerce widgets for building modern online stores.

From interactive product sliders and beautifully designed product grids to AJAX-powered filters, slide-in carts, header search, and wishlist functionality, Nexvio brings essential shopping interface components together in one flexible plugin.

Whether you're creating a new WooCommerce store or improving an existing storefront, Nexvio gives you practical design controls and shopping features directly inside Elementor.

Nexvio is an independent plugin and is not affiliated with or endorsed by WooCommerce or Elementor.

## 🧱 Elementor Widgets

Nexvio organizes its widgets into practical groups to help you build different sections of your WooCommerce store.

| Widget Group | Available Widgets |
|---|---|
| Products | Product Slider, Products Card, Products Card Layouts, Product Showcase |
| Shop | Products Archive, Products Archive Layouts, Product Filter, Filter Popup |
| Single Product | Related Products, Related Products Layouts, Add to Cart |
| Header | Header Search, Header Cart, Wishlist Icon |
| Cart | Cart Panel, Floating Cart |
| Wishlist | Wishlist Card, Wishlist Card Layouts |

Available widgets depend on the plugin configuration and the relevant WooCommerce and Elementor setup.

## 🎨 Product Card Customization

Nexvio includes 12 selectable product card designs for supported product grid and archive widgets.

Available controls may include:

- Layout design selection.
- Product image appearance.
- Card content and descriptions.
- Button styling.
- Product badges.
- Pagination.
- Empty product messages.
- Product gallery image on hover.
- Hover animation effect, duration, and easing.

### Hover Gallery Animation Effects

Supported configuration options include:

- Fade
- Zoom In
- Zoom Out
- Slide
- None

Products without a secondary gallery image continue to display their main product image.

## 🔎 Product Filter & Search

The Nexvio Product Filter helps shoppers narrow down product listings using configurable filtering fields.

### Supported Filter Types

- Keyword search.
- Price range.
- Product categories.
- Product attributes.
- Product tags.
- Stock availability.
- Product ratings.
- Sale products.
- Featured products.
- Sorting options.
- Products per page.

### Category Display Options

- Category list.
- Checkboxes.
- Dropdown.
- Buttons.
- Category counts.
- Collapsible subcategories.

### Attribute Display Options

- Checkboxes.
- Color circles.
- Image swatches.
- Size buttons.

### Filter Popup

The Filter Popup widget opens a side panel containing the product filters. You can configure its position, apply behavior, and active-filter counter using the available settings.

---

## 🛒 Side Cart & Shopping Experience

The Nexvio Side Cart provides access to the shopping cart without requiring customers to leave the current page.

Features include:

- Product quantity updates.
- Remove product controls.
- Coupon entry.
- Subtotal and discounts.
- Shipping and order totals.
- View Cart and Checkout actions.
- Configurable cart appearance.

Available appearance presets include **Luxury**, **Light**, and **Dark**.

The cart uses WooCommerce cart data and relies on the store's WooCommerce configuration for applicable pricing, shipping, coupons, and checkout behavior.

## ❤️ Built-in Wishlist

Nexvio includes wishlist functionality without requiring a separate wishlist plugin for its built-in features.

### Wishlist Features

- Logged-in customer wishlists.
- Optional guest wishlists.
- Cookie-based guest wishlist storage.
- Guest wishlist transfer after login.
- Configurable wishlist page.
- Guest wishlist duration settings.
- Wishlist card layouts.
- Wishlist counter.

### Wishlist Shortcode

Use the following shortcode where shortcodes are supported:

```text
[nexvio_wishlist_count]
```

This shortcode displays the wishlist item count.

## 🧩 WooCommerce Integration

Nexvio extends the WooCommerce shopping experience through Elementor widgets.

Depending on the widget, store configuration, and installed integrations, Nexvio can work with:

- WooCommerce products and product categories.
- Product prices and stock information.
- Product images and gallery images.
- Product attributes and variations.
- WooCommerce cart data.
- Related products.
- Product archives and pagination.
- WooCommerce shipping, coupons, and checkout links.

Payment gateways must be configured separately through WooCommerce. Any third-party payment-provider displays require the relevant supported integration.

## 🔒 Compatibility & Best Practices

Nexvio is designed to work with WordPress, WooCommerce, and Elementor.

For the best experience:

- Keep WordPress, WooCommerce, Elementor, and Nexvio updated.
- Configure WooCommerce products, categories, attributes, and images correctly.
- Test archive and filter configurations before publishing.
- Verify cart and checkout behavior on your own store.
- Back up your website before installing updates or changing important settings.

Actual compatibility depends on the installed versions and the configuration of your website.

## 📦 Installation

### Install from WordPress.org

1. Log in to your WordPress admin dashboard.
2. Navigate to **Plugins → Add New Plugin**.
3. Search for **Nexvio for WooCommerce**.
4. Click **Install Now**, then **Activate**.
5. Make sure WooCommerce and Elementor are installed and activated.
6. Open the **Nexvio** menu in your WordPress dashboard.
7. Review the dashboard and enable the widgets you want to use.
8. Open a page with Elementor and find the **Nexvio** widget category.
9. Add your preferred widgets and configure their content and styling.
